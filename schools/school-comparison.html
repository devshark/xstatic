Aldrich Bay GPS

愛秩序灣官立小學 · Shau Kei Wan

Strengths

  • Only school with Queen's College secondary nomination
  • Free + very low charges (~$290/yr)
  • Formal gifted student program
  • Multiple Intelligences Programme (structured)
  • 3 playgrounds — most outdoor space
  • Two Native English Teachers

Weaknesses

  • Newest school (est. 2000) — less tradition
  • Lowest postgrad teacher % (22%)
  • Further east — transport from NP/QBay

Academically driven families, especially those with boys aiming for elite secondary school. Best long-term pathway in this list.

Taikoo Primary School

太古小學 · Quarry Bay / Tai Koo

Strengths

  • Largest campus (~10,870 m²) by far
  • Best arts program: Chinese opera, orchestra, English musical
  • Cambridge courses available
  • Most experienced teachers (61 staff, 77% special ed trained)
  • Long track record (est. 1923 — oldest in list)
  • Corporate (Swire) backing — well-resourced

Weaknesses

  • No direct secondary pathway (SSPA)
  • Putonghua streaming can divide class cohorts

Families who value arts, performing arts, and a well-rounded experience on a large, well-resourced campus. Strong all-rounder.

Buddhist Chung Wah Kornhill Primary

佛教中華康山學校 · Quarry Bay

Strengths

  • Best special education support (82% trained)
  • Most flexible learning tracks (remedial + elite)
  • 20+ extracurricular activities
  • Robotics and drone courses (rare at primary level)
  • Progressive wellbeing: "Happy Wednesday", "English Monday"
  • 3 playgrounds

Weaknesses

  • Buddhist ethos — may not suit all families
  • Higher charges (~$480/yr)

Families who prioritise diverse extracurriculars, strong SEN support, and a progressive wellbeing approach. Also great for tech-curious kids (robotics/drones).

CMS Tanner Hill

丹拿山循道學校 · North Point

Strengths

  • Highest postgrad teacher qualification rate (45%)
  • Strong drama education (unusual at primary level)
  • Life education P1–P6 (holistic development)
  • Roof garden — unique outdoor space
  • Brass band, choir, dance

Weaknesses

  • Christian (Methodist) ethos — mandatory for families
  • Ability streaming from P4 can be pressure-inducing
  • Smaller school (21 classes) — fewer sports team options

Christian families who want a values-based education, strong teacher qualifications, and a focus on performing arts and holistic development.

North Point GPS

北角官立小學 · North Point

Strengths

  • Central North Point location — most accessible
  • Free + low charges (~$400/yr)
  • Government school reliability and oversight
  • Established 1954 — strong track record
  • 37% postgrad teachers (best among govt schools)

Weaknesses

  • Only 1 playground (least outdoor space)
  • Secondary pathway limited vs. Aldrich Bay
  • Fewer standout programs vs. peers

Families living in North Point who want a dependable, affordable government school with a long track record and no religious affiliation.

Chan's Creative School

啟基學校（港島）· North Point

Strengths

  • Smallest school — maximum individual attention
  • Lowest fees by far (~$80/yr)
  • Unique programs: fencing, aerodynamics
  • AR immersive learning room
  • Mixed ability throughout (no streaming pressure)

Weaknesses

  • Smallest peer network (10 classes total)
  • Fewest teachers (29) and lowest SEN training (46%)
  • Most exams from P1 (2 tests + 2 exams)
  • Lowest postgrad teacher rate (30%)

Families who prefer a very small, intimate school environment with unique hands-on programs. Good if child benefits from individual attention over large social environment.
